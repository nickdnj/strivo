# Code of Conduct

StriVo is a friendly project about watching TV. Let's keep it that way.

## Our pledge

We want participation in StriVo to be welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, whatever their experience level, background, identity or the TV they own.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and non-technical users.
- Assume good intent. Ask before assuming.
- Give and take feedback gracefully, about the work, not the person.
- Respect people's time: search before posting, and keep threads on topic.

## Not okay

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory jokes or language
- Posting someone else's private information
- Sharing ways to get around paywalls, DRM, or streaming services' terms
- Spam or self-promotion unrelated to StriVo

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock threads, or block people who don't follow this code. To report a problem privately, use GitHub's **Report content** option, or contact the maintainers through the repository's security contact.

This code is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
